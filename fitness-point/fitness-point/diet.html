Daily targets

Quick nutrition rules

Protein

Aim for roughly 1.6–2 g per kg bodyweight if training hard.

Water

3–4 litres a day, more in Goa's humidity.

Whole foods

Dal, eggs, fish, paneer, rice, fruit and veggies do the heavy lifting.

Cut the junk

Sugary drinks and deep-fried snacks are the easiest wins.
