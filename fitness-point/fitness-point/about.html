Our story

Fitness Point started with a simple idea: a good gym should be affordable, clean and run by people who know training. We're a unisex gym with certified trainers, helping members build healthy habits that last longer than a New Year resolution.

Whether you want to lose weight, put on muscle, prepare for sports or simply feel better, we meet you at your level.
