Is it a unisex gym?

Yes. Men and women train on the same floor, with certified trainers on hand.

Not compulsory, but we recommend at least a few sessions to learn form and set up a routine.

Admission is ₹500, with an extra 10% off for students. Check the Membership page for plan prices.

Yes, at ₹4,000 per month with a free diet plan included.

Do you give diet guidance?

Yes. Plans include diet direction, and personal training comes with a full diet plan.

Can I try before I join?

Ask at the gym about a trial session. Visit or call to arrange it.

Where are you located?

Below Magson Supermarket, opposite St Mary High School, Ponda, Goa.

What should I bring?

Water bottle, towel, comfortable clothes and proper shoes.

Can I freeze or transfer my membership?

Policies vary by plan. Ask the front desk before you join.
